- [Platformer](https://masiloh.itch.io/the-witchs-carrot) where you play as a rabbit trying to get a carrot, but a witch incrementally curses you to create new interactions with the same level
- Voted [4th](https://itch.io/jam/baudo-game-jam/rate/3984928) overall out of 72 games for the [BAUDO's Game Jam](https://itch.io/jam/baudo-game-jam)
- Around a dozen curses, 18 level combinations, settings and save system, animated art and backgrounds with shaders, and a secret level
